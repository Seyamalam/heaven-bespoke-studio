# Hackathon release and submission

Source: https://www.racdox.com/hackathon, read September 5, 2026.

- Deadline published: September 6, 2026, 11:59 PM BST. The timezone abbreviation is reproduced as published; verify with organizer if needed.
- Judging: UX/conversion 70%, design 20%, technical execution 10%.
- Fresh work; any tool allowed, including AI. Fully AI-generated work with no craft is disallowed. Keep design decisions, custom modeling source, QA evidence, and meaningful iteration visible.
- Publish a working URL and a public Facebook screen recording using `#racdox_hackathon`.
- FAQ suggests 30–90 seconds, without needing voice or face.
- Submit the Google Form linked from the hackathon page and join WhatsApp for updates.
- Winners announced September 8. Published terms say Heaven receives full ownership of the winning design/code for its use.

## Suggested recording

0–15s: hero and room switching. 15–40s: choose furniture, load 3D, rotate and change materials. 40–60s: save direction and open consultation, show selected details. 60–80s: mobile experience and showroom/contact details.

## Release gate

Complete QA, deploy, verify the public URL on mobile, then record. Confirm Facebook post is public and form links are correct. This build task does not itself post to social media or submit an entry.
