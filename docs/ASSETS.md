# Asset register

## Policy

Concept imagery is generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Furniture models are original procedural Blender designs. These are illustrations of a bespoke direction, not photos of completed Heaven projects or exact sellable products. Place concise disclosure near the collection/studio and in the footer. Never fabricate customer portraits, testimonials, showroom photographs, or craftsmanship claims beyond the brief.
