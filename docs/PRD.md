# Product requirements

## Objective

Help a prospective homeowner understand Heaven's bespoke offering, explore a personal furniture direction, and start a useful consultation in less than a minute.

## Audience and brand facts

Homeowners furnishing or improving homes in Chattogram. Heaven Furniture Mart was founded in 2020 by Abul Kalam Bhuiyan. The showroom is on Agrabad Access Road, Chattogram. Contact: +880 1960-481983; heavenfurnituremart@gmail.com. Free design consultation, bespoke work, premium materials, in-house craftsmanship, delivery and installation, and easy payment options are supported by the brief. No invented prices, warranties, reviews, delivery estimates, or certifications.

## Experience

1. Editorial hero immediately communicates bespoke furniture and offers a consultation.
2. Room selector changes large interior imagery, copy, and selected category.
3. Collection cards offer meaningful entry points to the design studio.
4. Interactive studio allows choosing sofa, lounge chair, or coffee table; rotating the model; changing fabric/wood; and inspecting dimension guides.
5. Save a direction locally and restore it on a later visit.
6. Consultation dialog gathers category, approximate room size, selected finish, name, and optional notes. It displays an editable message and opens WhatsApp only on an explicit user click.
7. Craft/material details, a three-step process, FAQs, and contact information reduce uncertainty.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every apparent control performs an action; no dead search/cart/account controls.
- One consistent primary CTA: Start your consultation.
- Works at 360px, 390px, tablet, and desktop without horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, properly managed modal focus and Escape dismissal.
- Reduced motion disables decorative animation and automatic motion.
- Content and inquiry remain available without WebGL; 3D loads on request.
- Local storage contains design preferences only; no personal form data is persisted.
- Generated imagery and furniture concepts are identified as illustrative.
- WhatsApp message includes configuration and does not promise that an inquiry was sent.
- Main thread and initial download remain small; defer 3D code and model downloads.

## Scope boundaries

One landing page with interactive sections. No checkout, payments, accounts, inventory, or fake booking backend. Public social posting and hackathon submission remain separate user actions.
